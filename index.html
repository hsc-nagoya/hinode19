Scope of the Meeting

The scope of this conference series is to report and discuss recent scientific results obtained from the Hinode satellite, as well as to explore future directions of solar physics research. The discussion on future missions is also included in the scope. In recent years, the feasibility studies and development of the next-generation solar missions, SOLAR-C and MUSE, have made significant progress. This conference will also provide an opportunity to discuss future solar observations, with a particular focus on these upcoming missions.

Venue

The meeting place will be Sakata-Hirata Hall in Higashiyama Campus, Nagoya University, Japan.
